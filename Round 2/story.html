Our story · Patiala, Punjab

Started in a bedroom. Built for a generation.

Hey, I am Aryaman Saldi. I am 20, from Patiala, Punjab, and I make clothes for people who care how they show up.

Patiala has a royal past: the old maharajas wore the best fabrics, sharpest turbans and boldest embroidery. I grew up around that heritage but never saw it in what people my age were wearing. At 17, I started thrifting jackets, restitching them and selling them to friends from my bedroom. My first drop was 12 pieces and sold out in a day.

I learned pattern cutting from a tailor uncle in Sheranwala Gate and fabrics from a mill owner who took a chance on a teenager with a spreadsheet. Today, Riyasat is a full label: premium fabrics, clean silhouettes and details made by local artisans.

Riyasat means a princely state — a kingdom. The idea is simple: old royalty, new rules. We take Patiala’s heritage and cut it in modern fits. Everything drops in small batches. When it is gone, it is gone.

Aryaman Saldi
Founder and Creative Director
