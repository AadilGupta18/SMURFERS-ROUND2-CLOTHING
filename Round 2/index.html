Founder portrait

Our story

From a bedroom in Patiala to a label with a point of view.

I started with a few thrifted jackets and a bedroom table. At 17, I was restitching pieces from Patiala’s old-world tailoring language and selling them to friends before I ever thought of a brand. The idea was simple: no one was making clothes for people like us.

Riyasat means a princely state — a kingdom. The name carries the grandeur of royal heritage while refusing to look stuck in the past. We blend the old-world drama of Patiala with new silhouettes, modern fits and a sharper edge.

Everything is produced in small batches, with details and embroidery finished by artisans in Patiala. We keep the label intimate, limited and built around the confidence of wearing something that feels personal.
